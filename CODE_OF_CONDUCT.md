# Code of conduct

Limitless welcomes contributors of every background and experience level.

## Working together

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and make room for questions.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's identifying information,
  credentials or private messages without their permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or post sexualized or abusive content.
- Respect maintainers' time and boundaries; keep discussions relevant to Limitless.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict
participation or block accounts, considering the severity and repeated nature of
the behavior. Corrections and good-faith disagreement are welcome.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's reporting tools to report abusive content or behavior. Avoid repeating
private information in a public issue. For vulnerabilities in Limitless itself,
use the private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
